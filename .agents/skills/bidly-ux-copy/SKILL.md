# Bidly UX copy

- Write natural, short Russian that sounds human and explains the next action.
- Avoid bureaucratic language, machine-translated phrasing, unnecessary anglicisms, hype, and promotional copy inside operational tasks.
- Never invent guarantees, scarcity, savings, supplier quality, availability, or “самую низкую цену”. Do not use dark patterns, disguised consent, confirmshaming, or urgency without a verified cause.
- State the complete price/period/conditions where material. Explain uncertainty and partial states plainly.
- For B2B, professional terminology is allowed when it is the clearest language for the audience.
- Put copy in the locale dictionary, not directly in JSX. Check wrapping and meaning on narrow screens and with long Russian text.
- Error text must say what happened, what remains safe, and what the user can do next without exposing internals or blaming them.
